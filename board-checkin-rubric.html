DomainA 4 looks like…Circle one
01Financial Transparency Every dollar of capital is accounted for: how much was spent, on what, and why. Nothing is estimated or hidden, and mistakes are owned openly. 4321
02Financial Statements Brings all three, current as of this week: a P&L (revenue, costs, profit/loss), budget vs. actual, and cash on hand. 4321
03Traction & Results Shows real numbers since the last check-in: customers, sales, units, or orders. Compares them to last time and explains what moved them. 4321
04Problem-Solving & Advisor Use Names the #1 bottleneck and its cause, says what was changed because of customer or board feedback, and brings one specific ask for the board. 4321
05Next-Step Goals 2–3 goals for the next check-in, each with a number and a date (“sell 40 units by 11/13”), not “grow sales.” 4321
06Presentation Skills Talks to the board, not the slides: eye contact, clear voice, no reading. Slides are clean and readable, and the pitch finishes on time. 4321
07Professionalism On time and set up before the board sits down. Dressed to job-interview standard, no phone out, materials printed or loaded. Respectful and owns the room. 4321
08Board Q&A Answers directly and briefly, backs answers with numbers, and says “I don’t know yet, I’ll follow up by ___” instead of guessing. 4321
Scores are a snapshot, not a verdict. The back of this sheet (the notes, promises kept, glows, and next steps) is what the student leaves with and what the next check-in builds on.
