Capstone & In-Person Days at a Glance

What our interns work on when they're not at their placement: the capstone they're building, and how we use our in-person class time to build it.

Michael Theriault · michael.theriault@bps101.net  |  Austun Savitski · austun.savitski@bps101.net

1The Capstone

Students present at a Capstone Breakfast in December for families, mentors and district leaders. It's 25% of their grade and has three parts.

On stage

Spotlight Talk

A prepared 1:30 talk: who they are, what they actually did, how they grew, and what's next.

In the lobby

Showcase Table

Guests walk up and get a short walkthrough. The table shows real work from the job and a board showing all 10 employability skills.

All semester

Portfolio

No set checklist. Students collect real evidence of their work in a Google Drive folder, saving one thing every week.

2Our in-person days

Students come back to class every other Tuesday for 90 minutes. Same three parts every time.

~25 min

Reflection

"Speed dating" in pairs: students rotate partners and answer questions about their week at work.

~35 min

Capstone work time

A 5-minute mini-lesson on one piece of the capstone, then time to build it.

~25 min

Employability skills

Students work through a skills packet on their own while we hold 1:1 check-ins.

Our best tip: have every student say their Spotlight talk out loud, timed, at least a week before the event. It catches almost every problem.
