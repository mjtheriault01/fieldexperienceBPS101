This is the one that can't wait

Applying today starts the clock on employer paperwork. Every day this slips is a day your start date slips with it. Absent today? Make this up within 48 hours — not "sometime."

What we do in class

  • 1Browse the placement list — all 7 career clusters, not just the obvious one.
  • 2Employer scavenger hunt: research your top 2–3. What do they do, who works there, what do they value?
  • 3Match the skills on your resume to the placements you want.
  • 4Meet with your teacher to talk through your choices and confirm your plan.
  • 5Submit your application. Sooner is better — see above.

After you apply

  • →Follow-up email 5–7 business days after applying if you haven't heard back. Brief, professional, confirms interest.
  • →Thank-you email within 24 hours of any interview. One paragraph, reference something specific from the conversation.
  • →Save both. They go in your portfolio as evidence of professional communication.
