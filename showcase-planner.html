Your table needs these five things

1
Skills Evidence Board BIGGEST PART
A big poster board (tri-fold or foam board) showing how you met each of the 10 employability skills during your internship: a photo or artifact and one "I showed this when…" sentence per skill. Plan it on the template first: fieldexperiencebps101.net/skills-board.html
Moments I already have (how many of 10?)
2
Something real from your work that guests can touch
An interactive piece you can point to and say "here's what I learned, look at this." Simple is fine. It has to be real and worth talking about.
  • Plumbing: pipe, fittings, a joint you made
  • Salon: foil or color samples, a styled mannequin
  • Engineering: a blueprint, CAD print, a part
  • Automotive: a worn vs. new part
  • Health/therapy: an exercise sheet or tool (no patient info)
  • Business/marketing: a piece you designed or a report

Setup expectations

  • Fully set up before doors open. Your setup is scored before guests arrive.
  • Professional dress, the same standard as your mock interview.
  • You staff the table the whole time and present to everyone who stops.
  • Label your artifacts. While you talk to one guest, two more are reading your table.
  • Ask your supervisor before you bring photos or anything from your workplace.
WHAT A STRONG TABLE LOOKS LIKE

Three Example Tables

Look for the same five things on every table: the Skills Evidence Board on an easel, a hands-on piece up front, a visual (board or device slideshow), the resume in a stand, and the table card. You can tell what each internship was from across the room.

Plumbing. Skills Board on the left, resume in a stand, laptop slideshow in the center, and a tray of real pipe, fittings, and a cutter that guests can pick up.
Salon. A styled mannequin, color swatches, and tools up front, with the board, resume, and tablet slideshow behind.
Engineering. A blueprint guests can lean over, CAD on the Chromebook, and real parts and calipers to handle.

AI-generated mockups to show the idea. Your table will look like your placement, not like these.
