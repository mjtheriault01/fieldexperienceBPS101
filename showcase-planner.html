WHAT A STRONG TABLE LOOKS LIKE

Three Example Tables

Every table has all five: (1) Skills Evidence Board, (2) a hands-on piece, (3) a visual, (4) resume, (5) table card. Square table, readable things in back, touchable things in front.

Plumbing. (1) Skills Board on the easel, (2) a tray of real pipe and fittings up front, (3) laptop slideshow, (4) resume in a stand, (5) table card at the front edge.
Salon. (1) Skills Board, (2) styled mannequin, color swatches, and tools, (3) tablet slideshow, (4) resume in a stand, (5) table card.
Engineering. (1) Skills Board, (2) blueprint, calipers, and real parts, (3) CAD on the Chromebook, (4) resume in a stand, (5) table card.
