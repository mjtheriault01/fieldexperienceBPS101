Skill Indicators

You already know the names of the 10 employability skills. This is about knowing what they actually look like — using the real BPS101 Performance Indicator Rubrics, the same ones your work is measured against.

How this works

Round 1 — Which skill is this? Read a short scene from a real workplace. Click the skill it demonstrates. No skill names are given — you have to recognize it.

Round 2 — Meets Expectation or Needs Practice? Now the skill and the specific indicator are labeled. Read the scene and decide which side of the rubric it lands on, before seeing the real rubric language for both sides.

Full Rubric Reference at the bottom has all 5 indicators for all 10 skills, in full — the complete rubric, not just the samples used above.

If you were absent: work through both rounds below, then go back to the Bootcamp page and submit your reflection to mark it complete.
