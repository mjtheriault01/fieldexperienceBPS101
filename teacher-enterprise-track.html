How Board Check-Ins work

A real investor-style update, run the same way every time so students know exactly what's coming.

Why it existsReplaces the site-supervisor evaluation. The board's score is the Enterprise cohort's Employer Feedback category (25% of the course grade), plus at least one client or advisor feedback form per grading period.
The boardMiss Savitski and Mr. Theriault, an administrator (Mr. Gamlin), and a community business partner. Rotate the guest seat so the board doesn't go stale.
FormatAbout 8 minutes of presentation and 5 minutes of board questions per student or team. Every founder speaks.
Students bringSlide deck • financial statements (P&L, budget vs. actual, cash on hand) • a promises report on last check-in's commitments.
ScoringEach board member fills out their own rubric. There are 8 domains, circled 4/3/2/1, for a total out of 32. Each member's total ÷ 8 gives a 4-point score, and those are averaged across the board. A 2+ level disagreement on any domain is talked through before the score is recorded.
The debriefRight after, the board and student fill in side 2 together: glows, and 2–3 specific commitments with a due date. The student photographs it. Those commitments become the Promises Kept? rows on the next check-in's rubric.
Dates#1 was Fri Sep 25. Remaining dates: TBA. The last check-in folds into the May capstone.
Brief guest board members on this before they score: a slow business is not automatically a 2. Traction & Results rewards numbers that are real and explained, not numbers that are big. A student who hustled into a hard market and reports it honestly has shown more than one who coasted on an easy one.

Run of show

The checklist for each round.

A week before
  • Send students the prep sheet and the date.
  • Book the room and confirm the guest board member.
  • Print one rubric per board member per student, two-sided.
  • Copy each student's last commitments into the Promises Kept? rows. Skip this for Check-In #1.
Day of
  • Brief the board on the scale and the slow-business rule above.
  • During the pitch, take notes on side 2.
  • Circle side 1 while the next student sets up.
  • Talk through any 2+ level disagreements before moving on.
Debrief & after
  • Fill in glows and 2–3 commitments with the student.
  • The student photographs side 2.
  • Average the board's scores and enter them as Employer Feedback.
  • Keep the sheets. They seed the next round's Promises Kept.
